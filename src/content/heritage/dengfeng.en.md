## The Past (I): A Cosmology of the Centre of Heaven and Earth

According to the *Rites of Zhou*, the Duke of Zhou measured the sun's shadow in the Mount Song region — "by the gnomon method, find the depth of the land and correct the sun's shadow, to locate the centre of the Earth." Dengfeng was thus honoured as the "Centre of Heaven and Earth." The three Han-dynasty stone gate-towers — Taishi, Shaoshi and Qimu — still stand today and rank among China's oldest surviving surface buildings; later emperors repeatedly ascended Mount Song for the fengshan sacrifices, and Wu Zetian herself performed the great rite in the Wansui Tongtian era.

## The Past (II): A Sacred Mountain of Three Teachings

At the foot of Mount Song, Buddhism, Daoism and Confucianism each left their masterpieces: the Northern Wei's Songyue Pagoda, the oldest surviving brick pagoda in China; Shaolin Temple, the cradle of Chan (Zen) Buddhism, whose legend of Bodhidharma facing the wall is known the world over; Songyang Academy, one of the four great academies of the Northern Song, where Cheng Hao and Cheng Yi lectured; and the Gaocheng Observatory built by Guo Shoujing in the Yuan dynasty, whose giant gnomon of more than 300,000 square inches measured the tropical year to a length almost identical to today's value.

## The Present: A Group of Eight Sites and Eleven Components

In 2010, the Historic Monuments of Dengfeng in "The Centre of Heaven and Earth" were inscribed under criteria (iii) and (vi), comprising 11 components in 8 groups: the Duke of Zhou's sun-shadow platform, the Gaocheng Observatory, the Songyue Pagoda, Shaolin Temple, Songyang Academy, the Zhongyue Temple, the three Han gate-towers and more. One property condenses the Chinese cosmology of "unity of heaven and man" and two thousand years of religious, educational and scientific history.

## Outstanding Universal Value (OUV)

- **Criterion (iii)**: The monuments present in full the enduring influence of the "Centre of Heaven and Earth" cosmology in ancient Chinese society.
- **Criterion (vi)**: Directly associated with the great traditions of Confucianism, Buddhism, Daoism and astronomical science, they are the material embodiment of core Chinese ideas.

## Facts at a Glance

- 11 components in 8 groups, inscribed in 2010
- The Songyue Pagoda is China's oldest surviving brick pagoda; the Gaocheng Observatory is a masterpiece of Yuan astronomy
