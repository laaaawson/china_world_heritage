## The Past (I): A Naxi Town Built Along the Water

The old town of Dayan in Lijiang was founded in the late Southern Song dynasty by the Naxi Mu clan chieftains. The town has no city wall — tradition says the Mu clan dreaded seeing the character "Mu" (wood) trapped within a "mouth" (the enclosing wall), and so built none. Three clear canals carry water from the Jade Dragon Snow Mountain into the town, threading every street and lane, so that every household faces the water and sleeps to its sound. The market at Sifang Street, the paving of Wuhua stone and the orderly water-taking customs at the three-eye wells make up the everyday life of the Naxi people living by the water.

## The Past (II): The Mu Mansion and a Frontier Trading Town

The Ming dynasty was Lijiang's golden age: the Mu chieftains managed the region for generations, and the Mu Mansion's "splendour rivalled that of a king's palace." At the same time, Lijiang stood on the main route between Yunnan and Tibet, with mule-caravan traders coming and going without cease, making it a major town on the Tea-Horse Road. Naxi Dongba culture also flourished here — the Dongba scriptures, written in pictographs, remain the only pictographic script still in use in the world today.

## The Present: A Town Reborn After the Earthquake

In 1996, an earthquake of magnitude 7 struck Lijiang and severely damaged the old town; it was rescued and restored to its original appearance, with the ancient streets, waterways and dwelling pattern intact. In 1997, the Old Town of Lijiang was inscribed on the World Heritage List under criteria (ii)(iv)(v). Today, Naxi ancient music sounds in the evening air at Sifang Street, and this frontier town beneath the snow mountain remains, in people's hearts, a place of "soft time."

## Outstanding Universal Value (OUV)

- **Criterion (ii)**: Lijiang fuses the urban planning wisdom of the Naxi, Han, Tibetan and other peoples.
- **Criterion (iv)**: The water-adjacent streets, waterways and timber-and-earth dwellings are an outstanding example of mountain town planning.
- **Criterion (v)**: The human habitat formed by the town, the snow mountain and the fields is an excellent model of harmony between people and nature.

## Facts at a Glance

- Founded in the late Southern Song; at an altitude of about 2,400 metres
- Inscribed in 1997; Naxi ancient music and the Dongba script are living cultural traditions
